## Why | Why I Do This

I studied Fine Arts before I studied design systems. That background keeps me connected to creative communities, and this work uses different muscles than product work.

Most emerging artists who want to sell work or build an audience face the same wall: they lack the visual infrastructure to look credible. ArtsGaze is a platform working to close that gap, and I joined as Design Lead to guide the visual identity and branding strategy.

## Approach | How I Approached It

### Mentoring two designers

I work with two designers on branding direction, storytelling, and engagement. My role is to give them ownership of decisions while keeping the work coherent. By choice, it's mostly design management.

Each artist collaboration starts with long conversations about their practice, inspirations, and audience. I treat every engagement as a mini brand strategy project: understand what the artist is actually trying to say before I design anything.

### A visual system for each artist

I build a flexible visual system for each artist: typography pairings, color palettes, layout templates, and production-ready files they can modify themselves.

It's the same approach I took with Orchid: a system people can extend without me.

### Designing for production costs

Every design accounts for manufacturing reality: printing methods, material costs, minimum order quantities. Artists operating on tight budgets can't afford a beautiful design that's impractical to produce.

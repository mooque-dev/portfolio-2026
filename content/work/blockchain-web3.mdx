I took on as many NFT and crypto projects as I could find in 2021 thinking it was the opportunity of a decade. The work was interesting. The ecosystem wore on me, and the year led to my first real burnout, which led to therapy, which over the years helped me build a stronger foundation than I had before.

The design work itself holds up, and so does what the year taught me about the space.

## Lesson | What the ecosystem taught me about abstraction

To design for Web3 at all, I had to learn its primitives properly: wallets and seed phrases, gas and why it fluctuates, what a smart contract actually commits you to, how L1s and L2s trade off. The lasting design lesson was that almost none of that vocabulary belongs in front of a user. Every project below dealt with the same question: how much of the machine to hide before trust breaks, and how much to show before people stop understanding it. The best crypto interfaces I studied behaved like good banking interfaces: the complexity is real, and it stays out of sight until you need to see it.

I've used the same instinct since. Making multi-entity accounting feel simple is the same job.

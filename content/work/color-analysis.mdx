## Idea | Live draping from your camera

Personal color analysis is normally a paid, in-person session: someone holds fabric to your face and you watch your skin either come alive or go grey. Most digital versions take a selfie and hand back a verdict, "you are an Autumn." I wanted you to see it on yourself, so the core interaction is live draping: tap a swatch and it recolors your outfit in real time, the same hold-it-up-and-look that makes the real thing work.

## The call | What I decided

The camera is the input, reading skin undertone and brightness off a live frame. The vision model runs entirely on your device, so nothing you show it ever leaves the browser. For something as personal as your own face, I wanted the image never to be sent at all.

## Question | What it tests

It also tests how much of a paid, gatekept ritual comes down to interaction design and a webcam.

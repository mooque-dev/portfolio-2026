This is where it started. Before product design, before UX, before design systems, I was making things because I wanted to see what I could make, no brief and no client, just curiosity and whatever software I'd just figured out how to open. Poly art, collage, typography exercises, album covers for friends. A lot of it was me figuring out what I liked, by making a lot of what I didn't.

Some of it holds up. Some of it was a phase. All of it was formative, and I'm not editing any of it out.

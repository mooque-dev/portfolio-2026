This is where it started. Before product design, before UX, before design systems, I was making things because I wanted to see what I could make. Poly art, collage, typography exercises, album covers for friends. A lot of it was just me figuring out what I liked.

Some of it holds up. Some of it was a phase. All of it was formative.

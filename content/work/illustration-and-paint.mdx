I wanted to be a children's book illustrator when I grew up. These are from that era. High school art classes, experiments with whatever materials I could find, and one sketch that made it into the Hamilton Spectator before I ever touched a computer for design.

The Monami pen one near the bottom is still the piece I'm most proud of making with my hands.

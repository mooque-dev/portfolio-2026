## Where it came from

Mooze is what [Vesta Press](/work/vesta-press) wanted to be. Same core belief —
reading and reflecting belong in one calm place — but built as a real,
signed-in product instead of a sketch.

## What I explored

- **Items and journal, together.** You save articles ("items"), read them in a
  focused view, and keep journal entries — all in one account, all searchable.
- **AI distillation.** A journal entry can be distilled with AI, turning a messy
  thought into something clearer to keep.
- **Built for keeping.** Accounts and sessions, a real database, file uploads,
  and a command palette to move quickly. It's designed to hold years of reading,
  not a weekend's.
- **Multilingual from the start.** Routing is locale-aware, so the whole app can
  speak more than one language.

## How it's built

Next.js with next-auth for accounts, Drizzle ORM over a Neon Postgres database,
and Vercel Blob for uploads. The Anthropic API powers the distillation; the data
model is simple and honest — users, sessions, items, journal entries.

The interesting part wasn't any single feature. It was taking a rough personal
tool and giving it the structure — auth, schema, i18n — that a thing you actually
rely on needs.

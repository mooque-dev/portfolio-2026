## Why it exists

In 2026 I joined a roughly forty-person amateur musical — well outside my comfort
zone, and a real test of keeping a big group on the same page. Coordinating who's
at which rehearsal, across months of practices building to performance, was the
kind of small, annoying problem that a small, focused tool solves better than a
group chat.

So I built one.

## What it does

- **A shared schedule** of practices and performances for the 2026 run.
- **Attendance tracking** for both rehearsals and shows, so it's clear at a glance
  who's in for each date.
- A clean, no-fuss interface — the people using it aren't software people, so it
  had to be obvious.

## How it's built

Next.js and React with TypeScript, backed by a Neon Postgres database. It's
deliberately small: one job, done well, for an actual group of real people who
needed it.

The point of this one wasn't ambition. It was designing for a non-technical
audience I happen to see every week — and watching whether they'd actually use it.

## Why I built it

I read a lot online and lose almost all of it. Vesta Press was a first attempt at
a calmer alternative to the read-it-later pile: a place to save something, read it
without the clutter, and actually write down what it made me think.

## What I explored

- **Save and clean.** Paste a link and it extracts the article into clean,
  readable markdown — no ads, no popovers, no chrome.
- **OCR.** Point it at an image of text and pull the words out, so a photo of a
  page becomes something you can read and keep.
- **A reader and a library.** A focused reading view, and a library of everything
  you've saved.
- **A journal beside it.** A writing space that sits next to your reading, so
  reflection isn't a separate app.

## How it's built

Next.js and React with TypeScript. Article extraction uses Readability and a
HTML-to-markdown pass; the Anthropic API handles the AI assistance; Supabase
stores everything. There's a clean export-to-markdown path so nothing you save is
trapped.

Vesta Press was the sketch. [Mooze](/work/mooze) is where I took the same idea and
built it out properly.

## Problem | Keeping every word the writer wrote

Vesta never summarizes. It strips the ads and the cramped columns and the popovers, keeps every word the writer put down, and only changes how those words are set.

Summarizing would have felt more like AI, but it would also have treated your time as worth more than the writer's.

## The call | What I decided

The typography changes with how you want to read. The same piece can be set as Editorial (magazine serif with drop caps), Minimal (clean sans and air), Manuscript, or Document, and switching between them is one tap. It takes a link, raw text, or a photo of a page, and once the words are pulled out it treats all three the same.

There is also a clean export to markdown, so your library is never locked in.

## Principle | Keeping the AI's job small

The hard part: clean up the page and leave the words alone.

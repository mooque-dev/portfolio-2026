# 7shifts: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.7shifts.com/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900; the Osano banner offered no decline button, so overlays were hidden for screenshots). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/7shifts-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Light and warm, with black feature blocks. Page `#FFFFFF`, warm off-whites `#FBFAF8` and `#F1F0EC`. Text `#000000`, secondary `rgba(0,0,0,.75)` and `#6E6D6C`. Dark sections pure `#000000` with white text. Black announcement bar on top.

## Type
- Display: **7sans** (custom, served as `medium`) 500. h1 64/57.6px, line-height 0.9, tracking -1.92px (-0.03em); h2 48/43px, -0.03em. A tight, slightly condensed grotesk.
- Text and UI: **Inter Tight** variable, 16px; buttons 16px 500.
- Handwriting: **Nanum Pen Script** for small eyebrows ("More than just scheduling") and emphasised numbers in orange.
- Google Fonts: `Inter Tight` (free, already used) at 600 with -0.03em tracking and 0.9 line-height for 7sans: same tight grotesk feel with closed apertures. `Nanum Pen Script` is free. Exact for the other two.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: `#4570FF` (bright cornflower blue), `#FFFFFF` text.
- Secondary CTA: `#F1F0EC` pill with black text.
- Orange `#FF6808`: hand-drawn underline swash under headline words, big stats on black, handwritten numbers.
- Lime `#C6FF94`: "New:" tag, tag pills, highlighted words on dark.
- Lavender `#DAD8FF` / `#EBDCFF` and pale blue `#D6E0FF`: content-type pills and schedule bars; forest `#244F47` as a deep card colour.

## Shape
- Buttons: full pill, 48px tall, `0 16px` padding (nav 43px).
- Cards and media: 20px radius; big sections use 40px rounded corners where black or blue blocks overlap the next section.
- Floating pill nav (white, about 40px radius) with a soft shadow `0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,.1)`.
- Tag pills: fully rounded pastel fills, no borders.

## Composition
Floating rounded nav. Centered hero: handwritten eyebrow, two-line 64px headline with an orange marker underline beneath the key phrase, one-line subhead, blue pill and a tiny "no credit card" note; below, a 20px-rounded full-width video of a phone in a restaurant with real schedule UI cards layered over it. Black section with tilted polaroid photos with doodle stickers, headline with a handwritten orange number, then a four-up orange stat row. Resources: three 20px cards with pastel tag pills. Footer on warm `#F1F0EC` with a 40px rounded top. Friendly, energetic, mid-high density.

## Signature details
- Orange hand-drawn underline swash under headline words.
- Handwritten Nanum Pen Script eyebrows and numbers mixed into grotesk headlines.
- Polaroid-style tilted photos with doodle stickers.
- Bright blue pills paired with warm off-white pills.
- Pastel tag pills (lavender, lime, blue) on content cards.

## For our page
1. Inter Tight 600 headline at 64px, line-height 0.9, -0.03em, centered, with an SVG orange `#FF6808` scribble underline under two words.
2. A Nanum Pen Script eyebrow above the headline.
3. `#4570FF` pill CTA with a `#F1F0EC` pill secondary; floating pill nav with soft shadow.
4. A black section with your own photos as tilted polaroids and a four-up stat row in orange.
5. Project cards at 20px radius with pastel tag pills (`#DAD8FF`, `#C6FF94`, `#D6E0FF`).
6. Footer on `#F1F0EC` with 40px rounded top corners.

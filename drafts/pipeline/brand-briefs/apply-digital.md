## Canvas
Light, off-white. Background `#FCFCFA`, text `#1A1B1C`, near-black `#090A0B`. Panels in cool greys `#D1D5DB` / `#C7C8CC`, pale aqua `#DBEDF2`, a dusty blue `#7DBACC` and a warm taupe `#988D83`. Dark surface `#282A33` (graphite with a blue tint).

## Type
- Display: **Archivo** 500, h2 64px with line-height 0.8 (51.2px) and tracking -2.56px (-0.04em); h3 48/38px, -1.92px. Very tight leading so lines nearly touch.
- Labels: **Kode Mono** 400, 16px, uppercase, inside braces or brackets: `{ HELLO, WE'RE APPLY }`, `[ RETAIL | CPG | MEDIA | SPORTS ]`.
- Text: **Wix Madefor Text** 400/500, 16/28px.
- Google Fonts: all three are free on Google Fonts (`Archivo`, `Kode Mono`, `Wix Madefor Text`). Exact match.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: `#282A33` background, `#EDEDEF` text, uppercase 14px 500, tracking 1.4px (0.1em).
- Signal orange `#FF4A08`: brace-wrapped key phrases in headlines and mono labels.
- Pale aqua `#DBEDF2` and dusty blue `#7DBACC`: section and card backgrounds.
- Taupe `#988D83`: alternate panel colour.

## Shape
- Buttons: hard square corners (`border-radius: 0`), 50px tall, `14px 24px` padding, 2px border same as fill. A chamfered (cut-corner) dark "GENERATE" chip appears in the collage art.
- Cards and images: square or 4px; no shadows except a 2px inset outline on focusable items.
- UI fragments use thin 1px lavender-grey outlines and square selection handles.

## Composition
Header with square dark "CONTACT US" button, then a full-bleed autoplay video hero (visually no headline; the h1 is for screen readers). Below, an orange mono label left and a large video right, then the brace headline on pale aqua. A wide collage section scatters product fragments: photos, a selection box with corner handles, a bar chart, "Running agents..." mono text, a chamfered button, all over a huge pale circle. Insights section: one wide feature card plus a three-up grid with big tight Archivo titles. Editorial agency feel, medium density.

## Signature details
- Key phrases wrapped in `{curly braces}` and coloured orange.
- Uppercase mono labels inside `{ }` or `[ ]`.
- Ultra-tight display leading (0.8) with -0.04em tracking.
- Square, uppercase, tracked dark buttons.
- Collage of design-tool artefacts (selection handles, cut-corner chips).

## For our page
1. Headline in Archivo 500 at 56 to 64px, line-height 0.85, tracking -0.04em, with one phrase as `{orange}` in `#FF4A08`.

3. Square `#282A33` button, uppercase, tracking 0.1em; no rounded corners anywhere.

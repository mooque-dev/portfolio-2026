# Ashby: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.ashbyhq.com/ (studied 2026-11-15, 1440x900, computed styles read in the browser).

## Canvas
Light. Page background `#FCFCFE` (near-white with a cool tint), content sits on white `#FFFFFF` cards. Heading ink `#141415`, secondary heading and strong body `#373739`, body `#717075` (warm grey). Hairlines `#DBDBDC`.

## Type
- One family everywhere: **TT Norms Pro**, a geometric grotesk with round bowls and a tall x-height.
- Display: h1 72/79px, weight **575**, tracking -2.88px (-0.04em). Section h2 36 to 44px, weight 575, tracking -0.025em. The in-between weight (575, 545) is the tell: firmer than regular, never bold.
- Text and UI: 16px, 400 for body, 545 for buttons and nav labels with -0.008em tracking.
- Google Fonts: **Figtree** (variable, so 575 renders) is the closest free match: geometric, round, similar x-height. Plus Jakarta Sans is the fallback.

## Colour
- Primary button: purple vertical gradient `#6857DB` to `#473BCE`, text `#FCFCFE`, inset 1px dark ring and a faint inner top highlight.
- Ink purple for links ("Learn more", "Read Our Story"): about `#473BCE`.
- Soft tinted chips: lavender, peach and pink washes behind short phrases.

## Shape
- Buttons and inputs: 6px radius, 36 to 48px tall, 16 to 24px padding. Inputs white with a 1px `#DBDBDC` border and a soft inset shadow.
- Cards: white, 16px radius, hairline shadow `0 0 1px rgb(20 20 21 / .18), 0 2px 3px -2px rgb(20 20 21 / .3)`, no border.
- Product UI shown as stacked floating panels with small status chips.

## Composition
Centered hero: one short, heavy headline, a two-line intro where key phrases are set darker and heavier than the rest of the sentence, an email field beside a purple button, then a two-row customer logo wall. Below, each section is a big white rounded card on the off-white page, text left and product panels right. Calm, roomy, medium density, very little colour outside purple.

## Signature details
- Mixed-weight sentences: the important words darker and heavier in a grey line.
- White 16px cards floating on `#FCFCFE` with hairline shadows instead of borders.
- Purple gradient button with an inset ring.
- Tinted phrase chips (lavender, peach, pink) for short benefit lines.
- Floating, slightly stacked product panels.

## For our page
1. Off-white `#FCFCFE` canvas, Figtree at weight 575 for the headline with -0.04em tracking.
2. Purple gradient primary button, 6px radius, inset ring; white bordered secondary.
3. Put the opening object on a white 16px card with the hairline shadow, like their product panels.
4. Use the mixed-weight idea in the headline: the lead clause in full ink, the rest a step lighter in grey.
5. No logo, wordmark, serif "A" tile or customer logos.

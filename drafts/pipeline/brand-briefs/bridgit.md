# Bridgit: visual brand brief

Source: https://gobridgit.com/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900; the banner only offered Accept/Settings, so it and the chat widget were hidden for screenshots). ## Canvas
Light, warm "cement". Page `#F7F7F6`, cards `#FFFFFF`, text `#000000`, secondary `#737373`. Large dark sections in "safety green" `#09333A` / `#08333A` with white text. Warm beige `#EFE8D6` behind product screenshots.

## Type
- One family: **DM Sans**. Display 300 (light), h1 58/65px, normal tracking; big section statements 58px 300. h2 inside data blocks 32px 700 in `#08333A`; h3 24px 500.
- Buttons 16px 500/600; nav 18px 500.
- Eyebrows: **DM Mono** 400, uppercase, about 18px, black ("BETTER TEAMS FOR BETTER OUTCOMES", "PROJECT PLANNING").
- Google Fonts: `DM Sans` and `DM Mono` are free. Exact match.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: safety green `#09333A` background, `#FFFFFF` text.
- Signal red-orange `#FA4D16`: progress dot on the tab rail, round arrow buttons on testimonials, small highlights.
- Sod green `#C3D4B1`: announcement bar link and "New report" chip, selected-state chips in product UI.
- Beige `#EFE8D6`: panels behind product screenshots.
- Gunmetal `#D0CFCB` / grey `#E6E7E9`: rules and quiet fills.

## Shape
- Buttons: full pill (`30px` to `9999px`), 54 to 60px tall, `18px 25px` padding.
- Cards: `16px` radius (the dominant radius, used over 80 times), white on dark green; screenshot panels 16px.
- Section shapes: dark green bands with very large rounded corners (`84px 84px 0 0` top, rounded bottoms too), making "arches".
- Shadows rare and soft (`0 7px 29px rgba(100,100,111,.2)`).

## Composition
Dark green announcement bar, light nav with a green pill CTA. Centered hero: mono eyebrow, light 58px headline on two lines, one pill. Then a dark green arch section with a large rounded white card: tab rail on top (labels with a dotted line and orange dot showing progress), card split into mono eyebrow + headline + paragraph + pill on one side and a product screenshot on a beige panel with dotted path lines on the other. Big centered light-weight trust statement, animated counter digits, and testimonials as split photo/quote cards with an orange round arrow. Spacious, calm, medium density.

## Signature details
- Dotted trail lines with an orange dot as progress and connectors.
- Uppercase DM Mono eyebrows over light DM Sans headlines.
- Deep teal-green sections with huge rounded corners.
- Product screenshots on warm beige panels.
- Rolling-digit animated counters.

## For our page
1. Page `#F7F7F6`, hero centered: DM Mono uppercase eyebrow, DM Sans 300 headline at 56px.
2. `#09333A` pill buttons, 56px tall, white text.
3. A dark `#09333A` section with 80px rounded top corners holding white 16px-radius project cards.
4. Put screenshots on `#EFE8D6` panels with a dotted SVG path wandering behind them.
5. A horizontal "chapter" rail with a dotted line and `#FA4D16` dot to show progress through case studies.
6. One rolling-number counter (projects shipped, years) in DM Sans 700 `#08333A`.

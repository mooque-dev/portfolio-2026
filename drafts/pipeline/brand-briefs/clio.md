# Clio: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.clio.com/ca/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900, cookies opted out). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/clio-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Light page with a dark hero. Page background `#FFFFFF`, alternating with warm greys `#F4F3F1` and `#EEEDEA` (also `#F7F5F5` "bone"). Body text `#404040`, headings `#04304C` ("mountain" navy), muted body on grey sections `#838383`. Hero and trust bands are deep navy `#0A2F4D` / `#04304C` with white text.

## Type
- Display: **Poppins** 400 (h1 50/62px, tracking -1px, about -0.02em); h2 30px 400; h3 Poppins **300** at 22px. Headings are regular or light, never bold.
- Text: **Source Sans Pro** 400/600, 16/23px; nav 14px 600.
- Eyebrows: uppercase, wide tracking, pale steel `#8FAABD`.
- Google Fonts: both are already free. Use `Poppins` (300/400) and `Source Sans 3` (the current name of Source Sans Pro). Exact match, no substitute needed.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: `#0069D1` background, `#FFFFFF` text, Source Sans 600.
- Navy `#04304C`: headings on light, full-bleed hero and "trusted" bands.
- Sky `#A8D4F4` (token `#A3DCFF`): large testimonial band with navy quote text.
- Bright blue `#2F97FF`: highlights and links on navy.
- Emerald `#008672`: flat colour panel behind product screenshots (also mint `#BADDAB`, coral `#D74417` in tokens, rarely used).

## Shape
- Buttons: full pill (`border-radius: 500px`), 45px tall, generous `10px 40px` padding, soft shadow `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.1)`. Secondary is a white 1px outline pill on navy.
- Product panels are square-cornered colour blocks; hero UI cards are rounded (about 12px) translucent glass with 1px light borders.
- Few shadows, no heavy borders.

## Composition
Split hero: left headline + paragraph + two pills, right a stack of translucent "glass" UI cards (notification, chat prompt, checklist) floating on navy. Below, a steady alternating rhythm on warm grey: short text block left (h3 + grey paragraph + blue "Explore" link with arrow), large real product screenshot right, set inside a flat emerald or sky panel. Big centered testimonial on a sky band, then a navy band with an uppercase eyebrow and light Poppins statement. Moderate density, lots of air.

## Signature details
- Light/regular Poppins headlines with slight negative tracking, never heavy.
- Product screenshots placed on flat, saturated colour panels (emerald, sky).
- Frosted glass UI cards stacked diagonally in the navy hero.
- Fat blue pill buttons with wide horizontal padding.
- Warm grey section backgrounds instead of pure white.

## For our page
1. Navy `#04304C` hero with Poppins 400 headline at about 50px, tracking -0.02em, white; Source Sans 3 body.
2. One `#0069D1` pill CTA (padding 10px 40px, soft shadow) plus a white outline pill.
3. Show each case study as a screenshot inside a flat emerald `#008672` or sky `#A8D4F4` panel, text block left.
4. Alternate `#FFFFFF` and `#F4F3F1` section backgrounds.
5. A centered quote on a sky `#A8D4F4` band, navy text, small round avatar.
6. Hero right side: 2 to 3 translucent cards (rgba white 8%, 1px white 30% border) summarising your outcomes.

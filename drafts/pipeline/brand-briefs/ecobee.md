# ecobee: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.ecobee.com/en-ca/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900, cookies declined). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/ecobee-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Light retail canvas. Background `#FFFFFF`, soft panels `#F9F9F9` and mint `#DEEDE8`. Headings `#2D2926` (warm charcoal, not black), body `#5E5958`, nav `#443F3F`. Full-bleed hero photos and video carry the darks.

## Type
- Display: **Tiempos** (Headline) 500, h2 36/48px and 28px, tracking tight by design. Used for hero and section headlines, always ending with a period ("Featured posts.").
- Text and UI: **Bastion**, a clean geometric-humanist sans, 500 for body (18/28px), 700 for buttons, 800 for small headings.
- Google Fonts: `Source Serif 4` at 600 (opsz 48 to 60) for Tiempos: a sturdy contemporary text serif with similar compact proportions and firm serifs. `Newsreader` 600 also works. For Bastion, `Figtree` 500/700: geometric with humanist touches, similar round bowls and friendly x-height.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: white pill `#FFFFFF` with `#2D2926` text and 2px `#2D2926` border (on photos the pill sits white on dark imagery).
- Link blue `#1169C8`: inline text links, underlined with a 1px inset bottom shadow rather than text-decoration.
- Mint `#DEEDE8`: soft section and card backgrounds.
- Deep forest green (photo-led, sampled `#00140B` to `#001910`) for the sustainability band, and deep night blue (`#002247` to `#003768`) in the hero imagery; ecobee's yellow-gold appears only in imagery and badges.

## Shape
- Buttons: pills, `border-radius: 39px`, 44px tall (64px for large), 2px solid charcoal border, bold 14px label.
- Content panels and images: square or very small radius (about 9px on cards), boxed to a 1200px column with full-bleed heroes.
- Shadows almost absent; borders are 2px charcoal on interactive items only.

## Composition
Full-bleed hero video/photo with left-aligned white serif headline, one paragraph, a bold line of short facts, and a single white pill. Then centered serif section titles with a grey one-line subtitle, two-up product/lifestyle images, a press-quote row (badge left, bold quote, blue underlined product links), a wide photo band with brand line and serif headline, a boxed dark-green image panel with white copy and pill, and a "Featured posts." blog grid. Mid density, retail rhythm, lots of photography of homes and devices.

## Signature details
- Serif headlines that end with a full stop.
- Lowercase, round, wide brand wordmark mood: friendly and calm.
- Outline pills with 2px charcoal borders.
- Blue links with a thin inset underline.
- Moody, warm home photography at dusk and night.

## For our page
1. Headlines in Source Serif 4 600, charcoal `#2D2926`, always ending with a period.
2. Body in Figtree 500 at 18/28px, `#5E5958`.
3. White pill buttons with 2px `#2D2926` border, 44px tall, bold 14px.
4. Inline links `#1169C8` with `box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 #1169C8`.
5. A mint `#DEEDE8` band for testimonials or "how I work".
6. One full-bleed warm photo (your own: a workspace at dusk) with a white serif line over it.

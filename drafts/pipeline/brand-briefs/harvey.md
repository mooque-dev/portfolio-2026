# Harvey: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.harvey.ai/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900; the cookie dialog could not be dismissed by declining, so it was hidden for screenshots). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/harvey-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Light, warm off-white with long black interludes. Background `#FAFAF9`, secondary panel `#F2F1F0`. Text `#0F0E0D` (warm black), secondary `#706D66`, faint `#8F8B85`. Dark sections `#0F0E0D` with `#FAFAF9` text, cards on dark `#1F1D1A`. Hairlines `#CCCAC6` / `#33312C` on dark.

## Type
- Display: **HarveySerif** 400, h1 72/75.6px, tracking -0.9px (about -0.0125em). Elegant transitional serif with moderate contrast, regular weight only.
- UI and text: **HarveySans** 400/500, a neutral grotesk; section h2s mix serif (32 to 48px) and sans 500 (32px). Nav and small labels 14px.
- Google Fonts: `Newsreader` 400 (display optical size) for the serif: transitional, moderate contrast and bracketed serifs, reads as editorial and legal. Or `Source Serif 4` at opsz 60. For the sans, `Inter` 400/500 or `Hanken Grotesk`, both neutral grotesks of similar width.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: `#0F0E0D` background, `#FAFAF9` text, 500 weight.
- Secondary: transparent with 1px `#0F0E0D` border (on dark: 1px `#FAFAF9`).
- Warm greys carry everything: `#706D66` copy, `#CCCAC6` rules, `#1F1D1A` dark cards.
- Accent: a cool slate-teal grey (sampled `#3D4849` to `#576263`, highlights `#CDD8D9`) in the textured art behind product UI; a tiny highlighter yellow `#F6F202` appears once. Essentially monochrome.

## Shape
- Buttons: `border-radius: 4px`, 48px tall hero CTA (32px in nav), `0 20px` padding.
- Media panels: 8px radius, full content width.
- No shadows; 1 to 2px borders in warm grey.

## Composition
Split hero: huge serif headline left, short sans paragraph and a black button right, top-aligned. Below, a full-width 8px-rounded panel with a painterly, grainy charcoal texture, holding a crisp product UI screenshot. Next, centered statement sections with graphite pencil-rendered 3D geometric shapes around floating UI cards. Black band with sans h2 left, outline button right, then a horizontal rail of large architectural photo video cards with serif overlay titles. Calm, spacious, low density.

## Signature details
- Big regular-weight serif headlines paired with a plain grotesk.
- Grainy charcoal / graphite pencil textures behind product UI.
- Near-monochrome warm palette; black buttons with small radius.
- Product UI framed inside an art panel rather than a device.
- Long black sections with architectural photography.

## For our page
1. Background `#FAFAF9`, text `#0F0E0D`, headline in Newsreader 400 at 64 to 72px, tracking -0.0125em.
2. Split hero: headline left, 18px Inter paragraph and black `#0F0E0D` 4px-radius button right.
3. Present each project screenshot inside an 8px-rounded panel with a subtle noise/grain texture (CSS SVG noise over `#576263` or `#CCCAC6`).
4. Use one full-black `#0F0E0D` section for selected work, with serif titles over darkened images.
5. No colour accents; let type, grain and spacing carry it.

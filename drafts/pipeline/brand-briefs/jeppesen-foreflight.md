## Canvas
Light pages framed by deep navy and full-bleed sky photography.
- Corporate: background `#FFFFFF`, light grey `#EEEFF0`, text navy `#001629`, body `#333333`.
- ForeFlight: header and dark bands `#212E39` / `#1F272E`, light sections `#F1F5F8`, body text `#565758`, headings `#212E39`. Hero photos sit under a deep blue tint (sampled `#093F61`).

## Type
- Corporate: **Proxima Nova** 300 to 800 for text and the hero (bold, centered); headings in **Neue Haas Grotesk Display** 500 to 700 at 36px with tight leading; eyebrows Neue Haas 16px uppercase, tracking 0.8px, orange.
- ForeFlight: **Gesta** (humanist sans) bold/black, uppercase with 1px tracking for h1 (40/48) and labels; mixed case 34px for section titles. Body Proxima Nova 18/30.6px (airy leading).
- Google Fonts: `Figtree` 400/700 for Proxima Nova (geometric-humanist, similar round "a" and proportions; less wide than Montserrat). `Inter Tight` 600/700 for Neue Haas Grotesk Display (neo-grotesk, tight). `Fira Sans` 700/800 uppercase, tracking 0.06em, for Gesta (sturdy humanist with technical feel).

## Colour
- Corporate primary CTA: orange `#FF791A`, `#FFFFFF` text.
- Corporate secondary: dawn teal `#006379` (news headlines, "Find your role" button); light teal `#19A2AE` in tokens.
- ForeFlight primary CTA: sky blue `#2C97DE`, `#FFFFFF` text; ghost version 2px `#2C97DE` border.
- Navy `#001629` (corporate) and slate navy `#212E39` (ForeFlight) for type and headers.
- Link blue `#0698D9` on corporate.

## Shape
- Corporate buttons: full pill (`48px`), uppercase 12.8px 700, tracking 1.28px (0.1em), `12px 25px` padding. Images 16px radius.
- ForeFlight buttons: near square `3px`, uppercase, tracking 1px, 46px tall, `16px 30px`. Cards about 6 to 8px radius.
- Shadows minimal (`0 0 18px rgba(0,0,0,.2)` on menus).

## Composition
Corporate: full-bleed sunset-from-the-wing photo with transparent nav, centered two-line white headline; then a centered brand row, split careers block (orange eyebrow, bold title, paragraph, teal pill) with a rounded photo, and a three-column news list in teal with uppercase dates. ForeFlight: hero carousel of cockpit photos with an iPad showing the app, left-aligned uppercase headline, paragraph, app-store badge, carousel dots; then centered section titles and four-up cards that are blue-tinted duotone photos with white uppercase labels; alternating dark photo bands with tracked uppercase sub-heads and stacked blue buttons. Clean, aviation-practical, medium density.

## Signature details
- Sky and wing photography, dusk gradients, cockpit iPads.
- Uppercase, letter-spaced buttons and labels.
- Blue duotone overlays on photo cards.
- Orange as the single warm accent against navy and teal.
- Dates and metadata in small tracked caps.

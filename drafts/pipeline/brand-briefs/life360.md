# Life360: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.life360.com/en-ca (studied 2026-11-15, 1440x900, cookie banner left alone, nothing accepted).

## Canvas
Light, warm retail canvas. Background `#FFFFFF`, warm off-white panels `#F9F7F3` and `#F4F0E7`, and a cream hero panel `#F3EFE8` boxed with a 16px radius inside the page gutter. Headings and body ink `#130F0C` (a warm near-black). Dark charcoal `#31302E` for footer and dark strips. Product photography (phones, trackers, pets, keys) carries the colour.

## Type
- Display and text: **CentraNo2**, a geometric grotesk. Headlines at 900 (h1 60/69px, section h2 72 to 96px), normal tracking, sentence case ending in a full stop ("Stay connected to everything you love."). Body 400 at 24/32px under the hero headline, 16px elsewhere.
- Google Fonts: `Outfit` 800 for the display (geometric, round bowls, heavy weights read close to Centra Black). `DM Sans` 400/700 for text: geometric with a friendly x-height.

## Colour
- Primary purple `#762EEB`: filled buttons with white text, and the outline button's border and label. Lighter purple `#8652FF` and a deep night purple `#180D43` appear in brand moments.
- Signal yellow `#FFCE19` for the promo strip across the top.
- Fresh green `#89EC7E` for small badges and highlights.
- Warm greys `#C5C4BF`, `#51504B` for secondary text and rules.

## Shape
- Buttons: 6px radius, 44px tall, 2px border, bold 16px label. Primary is solid purple; secondary is white with a purple border and purple label. Nav links are plain.
- Panels and photo cards: 16px radius, no shadow, warm cream fill.
- Very few borders; separation comes from cream panels on white.

## Composition
A boxed cream hero: heavy black headline left, one friendly paragraph, two buttons side by side, product shots (phone with a family map, trackers, keys) spilling right. Then a big centered headline with a playful aside, a three-up row of tall photo cards with white headlines over them, and large-number social proof ("Loved by 107 million people"). Generous, warm, family-friendly, a little cheeky in voice.

## Signature details
- Heavy geometric headlines that end with a full stop.
- The cream boxed hero panel with a 16px radius.
- Solid purple 6px buttons paired with a purple-outline twin.
- A playful aside in body copy (parentheses, asterisks).
- Yellow promo strip.

## For our page
1. Headlines in Outfit 800, `#130F0C`, ending with a full stop; one phrase in purple.
2. Body in DM Sans, warm grey `#51504B` for secondary.
3. Purple `#762EEB` buttons with a 6px radius.
4. A cream `#F3EFE8` intro band, kept light in dark mode.
5. The opening object as a white card with a 16px radius on the cream band, purple for the live state, yellow only for the benchmark line, green for "shipped".
6. No logo, ring mark, map pins or family avatars.

# OpenLoop: visual brand brief

Source: https://openloophealth.com/ (studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900, no decline option was shown). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/openloop-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Light page with a dark magenta hero. Body background `#FFFFFF`, alternate panels `#F7F7F8`. Text `#0F1125` / `#1A1A1B`, body copy `#404040`. Hero is a vertical gradient from near-black `#0E0008` / `#17030B` to deep berry `#8A0248` and `#A1044E` at the bottom, with white headline and pinkish body `#E7DBE0`.

## Type
- Display: **Archivo** 700 (also 800 and 700 italic), h1 52/55px tracking -1.04px (-0.02em); h2 48 to 56px.
- Accent words in the headline switch to **Archivo 700 italic** in pink (for example the last two words of the hero line).
- Text: **Inter** 400/500/600, lead 20.5/28.7px, nav 16px 500.
- Eyebrows and stat labels: Inter 600, uppercase, tracking about 0.12em, pink or white.
- Google Fonts: `Archivo` and `Inter` are both free. Exact match.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF4276 0%, #D40530 100%)`, `#FFFFFF` text, with a pink glow shadow.
- Brand pink `#E90153` / `#DE005D`: italic accent words, eyebrows, icons, plus-signs on stats.
- Berry `#8A0248`: gradient ends, dark product panels.
- Light pink `#FF8094` / `#FB7C91`: highlights and borders on dark.
- Teal `#3DB09C` appears rarely as a status colour.

## Shape
- Buttons: full pill (`999px`), 51px tall, `15px 32px` padding, Inter 600 17px, arrow icon; shadow `0 14px 34px -12px rgba(255,46,106,.6)`.
- Cards: 18 to 26px radius; dark device panels at about 26px; inner chips 12px.
- Shadows: soft `0 8px 24px rgba(15,17,37,.06)` on light cards, pink glow `0 24px 64px -20px rgba(222,0,93,.32)` on hero objects.
- Hero block ends with large rounded bottom corners (about 60px). Nav becomes a floating white pill on scroll.

## Composition
Split hero: left headline, lead paragraph, gradient pill; under it a three-up stat row (big number with pink "+", uppercase label, vertical dividers). Right: a phone-sized product card plus a vertical list of platform capability chips. Press logos on white. Then centered "HOW IT WORKS" eyebrow and headline with italic pink words, followed by alternating rows: dark rounded device panel with magenta glow on one side, app-icon tile (pink gradient squircle with white line icon), pink eyebrow, headline with italic pink tail, paragraph, pill on the other. High polish, medium density.

## Signature details
- Headlines whose last words turn pink and italic.
- Black-to-berry gradient hero with rounded bottom corners.
- Gradient pink pills with a glow and arrow.
- Pink gradient squircle icon tiles.
- Stats with a pink "+" and uppercase tracked captions.

## For our page
1. Hero gradient `#0E0008` to `#8A0248`, Archivo 700 white headline with the final phrase in Archivo italic `#E90153`.
2. Gradient pill CTA `#FF4276` to `#D40530` with arrow and pink glow.
3. Stat row under the hero: big numbers with pink "+", uppercase Inter 600 captions, 1px white/20% dividers.
4. Alternate project rows: dark 26px-radius screenshot panel with soft magenta glow beside pink eyebrow + headline.
5. 60px squircle tiles with a pink gradient and white line icons for skills.
6. Round the hero block's bottom corners at 60px.

# Pawlicy Advisor: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.pawlicy.com/ (studied 2026-11-15, 1440x900; no cookie banner appeared). Measured with computed styles.

## Canvas
Light, friendly retail canvas. Background `#FFFFFF`, inputs and soft fields `#F6F6F6` with a `#E0DFDE` hairline. Headings and body ink are a warm charcoal `#403E3C`, secondary text `#666563`. A slim slate-blue bar `#3F6587` under the nav speaks to vet teams. One big rounded orange panel (`#E26C33`, 24px corners) carries the "pet owners helped" number in white.

## Type
- Everything is **Nunito Sans**: h1 43px at 400 with one word in 900 ("instantly."), section h2 36px at 500, small h3 21px at 700, body 16/24 at 500, tracking a hair tight (-0.07px).
- Google Fonts: `Nunito Sans` itself. No second face.
- The wordmark is a serif with a heart-and-paw mark. Not used.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: orange pill `#E26C33`, white label, 42px tall, `border-radius: 9999px`, 8px 32px padding.
- Orange also picks out one phrase in a headline ("easy as 1, 2, 3").
- Slate blue `#3F6587` (and `#385A7A`) for links, small labels and the vet bar.
- Illustrated, cheerful cats, dogs and their people; carrier logos scattered around a big heart.

## Shape
- Pills for every button. Cards and fields at 8px, feature panels 16 to 24px.
- Light borders, very soft shadows, lots of white space.

## Composition
Centered hero: small slate label, a two-line headline with one bold word, a bold subhead with one italic word, then a Dog / Cat choice in two bordered tiles, a zip field and an orange "Compare Quotes" pill. Below: an illustration of carriers around a heart, a numbered 1-2-3 explainer in big orange outlined circles, the orange stat panel with polaroid-style illustrations, comparison sections and an FAQ.

## Signature details
- One phrase of a headline set in orange.
- Orange pill buttons with an arrow.
- Big rounded tiles for the first choice (Dog or Cat).
- Warm charcoal ink, never black.
- Cheerful flat illustrations of pets and owners.

## For our page
1. Nunito Sans throughout: h1 at 400 with warm charcoal `#403E3C`, h2 at 600.
2. One phrase of the headline in a deeper orange `#B9501C` (the brand orange at `#E26C33` is too faint for white text; the deeper one passes AA).
3. Pill buttons, deeper orange fill, white label.
4. The opening object as a sample quote card: soft `#F6F6F6` fields, 16px corners, pill toggles, numbers in tabular figures.
5. No logos, no carrier names, no illustrations copied.

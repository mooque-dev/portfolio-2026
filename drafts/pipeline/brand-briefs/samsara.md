# Samsara: visual brand brief

Source: https://www.samsara.com/ (redirects to /ca; studied 2026-10-22, 1440x900, "Necessary Only" cookies). Screenshots: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-mooque-Claude-arnd/efda517d-9b7b-4d0f-9f1f-93fc1f66b813/scratchpad/brand-shots/samsara-1..3.png`.

## Canvas
Mixed, leaning dark. Warm near-black `#1C1917` (and `#221F1B`) for hero overlays and full-bleed bands; light sections are white `#FFFFFF` and warm paper `#F5F4F1` (also `#E0DDD5`). Text `#1C1917` on light, `#FFFFFF` on dark, muted `#9C9890`. Every grey is warm (stone), never blue.

## Type
- Display: **Samsara Sans** 700, a very wide, heavy, squared grotesk, set UPPERCASE at 72/72px (line-height 1.0) for the hero only.
- Headings and text: **FK Grotesk** 700 for h2 (52/52px, tight, sentence case), 500 for lead text (24px), 400 body.
- Labels: **FK Grotesk Mono** 500/700, uppercase, wide tracking, wrapped in brackets: `[ OUR APPROACH ]`.
- Annotations: **Samsara Script**, a slanted all-caps marker hand.
- Google Fonts: `Archivo` at width 125 and weight 800 to 900 for the wide caps display (the variable `wdth` axis gets the same stretched, blocky stance). `Schibsted Grotesk` 500/700 for FK Grotesk (similar neo-grotesk with slightly quirky details and compact caps). `JetBrains Mono` or `DM Mono` 500 for the bracket labels. `Caveat` 500 uppercase for the marker notes.

## Colour
- Primary CTA: amber `#FEAE0F` background, `#1C1917` text (dark text on yellow, never white).
- Secondary CTA: solid white `#FFFFFF` with `#1C1917` text, or 1px `#1C1917` outline.
- Warm black `#1C1917` / `#403D37`: bands, borders, the floating nav border.
- Paper `#F5F4F1`: nav bar fill and resource sections.
- Token accents used sparingly in product art: emerald `#01B665`, blue `#2A75D4`, orange `#FF6319`.

## Shape
- Buttons: nearly square, `border-radius: 4px`, 48px tall, `12px 16px` padding, 700 weight, trailing arrow.
- Cards: square or 2 to 4px corners; no shadows. 1px solid dark or amber borders.
- Nav is an inset floating bar (light paper, small radius) sitting 16px in from the viewport edges.

## Composition
Full-bleed aerial industrial photo/video hero (ports, trucks) darkened, with centered giant uppercase headline, one line of lead text, two buttons. Then a white "approach" section: bracket eyebrow, bold two-line h2, short paragraph, amber button left; right a hand-drawn circular loop diagram with marker labels and arrows. Dark band with bold white h2 for proof. Resource cards are report covers on a graph-paper grid with spiral binding. Bold, confident, mid density.

## Signature details
- Amber-yellow buttons with near-black text and a right arrow.
- `[ BRACKETED MONO ]` uppercase eyebrows.
- Hand-drawn marker annotations and sketchy arrows over clean layouts.
- Graph-paper / notebook textures behind cards.
- Wide, heavy all-caps hero headline on a darkened field photo.

## For our page
1. Warm-black `#1C1917` hero, headline in `Archivo` wdth 125 / 900, uppercase, line-height 1.0.
2. Amber `#FEAE0F` CTA with `#1C1917` text, 4px radius, arrow glyph.
3. Section eyebrows as `[ CASE STUDY ]` in DM Mono uppercase, tracking 0.12em.
4. Annotate one diagram or screenshot with Caveat uppercase notes and hand-drawn SVG arrows.
5. Put project cards on a faint 24px graph-paper grid over `#F5F4F1`, square corners, no shadow.
6. Keep all greys warm (stone), never cool slate.
